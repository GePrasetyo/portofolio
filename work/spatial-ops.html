2022 – present Tech Lead · Gameplay Programmer

Spatial Ops

A high-fidelity mixed-reality shooter that maps your real room and turns it into a co-located multiplayer arena. Winner of the Auggie Award for Best Game 2025. I joined as a gameplay programmer, then ran technical production for an eight-engineer team through launch and live updates on Meta Quest, Steam, Pico and Apple Vision Pro.

01As Tech Lead

  • Production and delivery. Directed end-to-end technical production: milestone planning, sprint cycles and daily task distribution for an 8-engineer team, from prototype to release and live updates.
  • Vendors and co-dev. Managed deliverables and milestones for outsourced QA, localization co-development studios and contract audio and video engineers.
  • Cross-functional alignment. Primary technical bridge across 10+ internal departments and external partners, coordinating dependencies and feature integration from prototype to final release.
  • Technical PR and media. Secure build distribution, technical access and direct troubleshooting for media partners, influencers and PR stakeholders during review cycles.
  • Feasibility and scope. Defined feature roadmaps with the Creative Lead, evaluated technical viability, and mitigated risks and bottlenecks before they impacted production timelines.
  • Backend health and live monitoring. Monitored third-party multiplayer backend health (Photon) alongside internal telemetry and crash reporting, ensuring stable uptime and rapid resolution of live outages.
  • Pipeline and tooling. Championed custom team tooling, the Bot State Machine Editor and Campaign Event Designer, to empower designers and streamline content creation.
  • QA and process automation. Spearheaded the integration of LLM-driven automated playtesting agents and AI workflows (Claude, Unity CLI) into the native pipeline, accelerating QA iteration and root-cause bug analysis for the team.

02As Programmer

  • Photon Fusion 1 integration, later migrated to Fusion 2.1.
  • 3D grid A* pathfinding optimised for flying AI navigating real room geometry from spatial mapping data.
  • Custom editor tools: Bot State Machine Editor on Animator StateMachineBehaviours, and a Campaign Event Designer for levels, Timeline cutscenes and enemy encounters.
  • Hitscan and projectile weapons, damage system, ragdoll hit reactions, HUD.
  • UniTask for high-performance async gameplay code. Addressables for map event themes and asset streaming.
  • Telemetry analytics and Unity crash/exception reporting for live game health.
  • Stability and memory work with Unity Profiler, Memory Profiler, Project Auditor, Profile Analyzer and Xcode for native crashes.
  • Unity Localization.
